Our Philosophy

Space Isn't Just for Government Astronauts.

Founded in 1996, the Space Tourism Society champions a bold premise: for humanity to truly become a multi-planet species, space must become a vibrant destination for hospitality, entertainment, architecture, sports, art, and everyday human culture.

While rocket engineers solve propulsion, STS brings together visionary architects, theme-park imagineers, cruise-line executives, filmmakers, and policy leaders to design what people actually do once they arrive in orbit and on the Moon.
